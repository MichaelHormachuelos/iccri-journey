# ICCRI – Journey of Discovery

## Year 1 (2026–2027)

Welcome to the **Year 1 Journey of Discovery** of the **Idea-Centric Computing Research Initiative (ICCRI)**.

This repository documents the first year of ICCRI's development—from the earliest ideas and research discussions to the creation of prototypes, publications, software architecture, and the public research platform.

Unlike traditional project documentation that focuses only on completed work, this journal records the journey itself. It captures the questions we asked, the assumptions we challenged, the milestones we reached, the obstacles we encountered, and the lessons we learned along the way.

The purpose of this journal is threefold:

* **Document** the evolution of ICCRI and the field of Idea-Centric Computing.
* **Share** the research and development process openly with the community.
* **Preserve** the historical record of how a new research initiative was built from the ground up.

## What You'll Find

Each daily entry may include:

* Research and development milestones
* Website and software progress
* Architectural decisions
* Research insights
* Lessons learned
* Founder reflections
* Challenges and breakthroughs
* Community and collaboration updates

Every entry represents one step in the ongoing journey toward advancing **Idea-Centric Computing**.

## Guiding Philosophy

We believe that meaningful innovation is built through consistent progress rather than isolated breakthroughs. By documenting the process openly, we hope to contribute not only our research outcomes but also the thinking, experimentation, and continuous learning that shape them.

## Year 1 Goal

The primary objective of Year 1 is to establish the foundations of ICCRI by:

* Defining the theoretical framework of Idea-Centric Computing
* Publishing foundational monographs and research documents
* Developing the ICCRI website and public research platform
* Building the initial software architecture and prototypes
* Growing a community of researchers, educators, developers, and collaborators

## About ICCRI

The **Idea-Centric Computing Research Initiative (ICCRI)** is an independent research initiative dedicated to advancing the emerging field of **Idea-Centric Computing**. Through open research, reference architectures, software prototypes, and interdisciplinary collaboration, ICCRI explores new ways of designing computing systems that place ideas—rather than files or applications—at the center of the digital experience.
